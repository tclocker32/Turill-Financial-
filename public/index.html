Concentrated Stock Strategy

Your biggest winner can become your biggest financial risk.

Selling everything can trigger a major tax bill. Doing nothing can leave your future dependent on one company. The answer is often a coordinated combination of liquidity, diversification, tax planning, hedging, charitable planning, and disciplined exit strategies.
