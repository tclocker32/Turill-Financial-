Common Questions

Do I have to become an ongoing client?

No. The Blueprint is a standalone planning engagement. You may implement the recommendations yourself or seek help elsewhere.

Can I use my current advisor or tax professional?

Yes. The Blueprint can help organize the decisions and questions you may wish to coordinate with your existing professionals.

Is investment management included?

No. The $2,000 fee covers the planning engagement described on this page. Ongoing advisory or implementation services require a separate agreement.

When does the 30-day timeline begin?

The targeted timeline begins after payment and receipt of the information reasonably required to complete the planning work.

Does the plan include legal or tax advice?

No. The plan may identify tax or estate-planning considerations, but you should consult qualified tax and legal professionals before acting on those matters.

No. Financial projections are estimates based on assumptions and the information provided. They are not guarantees of future results.
