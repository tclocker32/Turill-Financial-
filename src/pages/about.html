About Us

Planning before products.

Turill Financial is a planning-first practice. We start with the decision you are actually facing—not with a product that needs to be sold—and work outward until the investment strategy, the tax picture, the risk exposure and the estate plan all point in the same direction.

Why I Do This

Financial planning is another form of coaching.

Growing up as the only child of a single mother, I saw what financial uncertainty could do to a family. I learned to save early, but nobody taught me what came after saving.

Years later, after coaching high school football and track and building a performance business, I realized the work I loved most was helping people see where they were, understand where they wanted to go and build a plan to close the gap.

Today I apply that same mindset to wealth. My role is not simply to manage assets. It is to help clients understand what their money is intended to do, what risks they are taking and why each part of the strategy exists.

What We Believe

Four principles that shape every recommendation.

Diagnosis before prescription

A recommendation made before the full picture is understood is a guess. We map the accounts, the tax exposure, the obligations and the goals first.

Coordination over collection

Most people do not have a bad plan. They have five reasonable plans that were never introduced to each other. Our work is making them agree.

Tradeoffs made visible

Every meaningful financial decision costs something. We model the alternatives so you can see what you are trading away before you commit.

Durability over prediction

We do not build strategies that require the next forecast to be right. We build strategies that hold up across markets, tax rules and career changes.
